## Procedure

1. Thawed 5X Ultra Low First-Strand Buffer at room temperature; thawed TCR dT Primer, TCR SMART UMI Oligo, RNase Inhibitor, and Nuclease-Free Water on ice.
2. Prepared Lysis/Inhibitor Mix by combining 19 µl 10X Lysis Buffer with 1 µl RNase Inhibitor.
3. Added 1 µl Lysis/Inhibitor Mix to 9.5 µl of purified RNA (1000 ng input) in a 0.2 ml PCR tube, for a total reaction volume of 10.5 µl.
4. Added 2 µl TCR dT Primer (12 µM) to the sample, mixed by vortexing, and spun down briefly.
5. Incubated the tube at 72°C for 3 min, then at 4°C for 2 min in a preheated thermal cycler.
6. Prepared Master Mix per reaction: 4 µl 5X Ultra Low First-Strand Buffer, 1 µl TCR SMART UMI Oligo (48 µM), 0.5 µl RNase Inhibitor, and 2 µl SMARTScribe Reverse Transcriptase (100 U/µl), for a total of 7.5 µl.
7. Added 7.5 µl Master Mix to the reaction tube and mixed by gentle pipetting.
8. Incubated the reaction in a preheated thermal cycler at 42°C for 180 min, followed by 70°C for 10 min, then held at 4°C (overnight).
